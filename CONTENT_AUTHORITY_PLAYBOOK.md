# Sanjay Nursery v1.4.0 — Content Authority Playbook

## Publishing principle

Publish fewer pages with nursery evidence rather than many near-duplicate pages. The CMS quality gate is designed to require useful structure, search intent, an identifiable reviewer, first-hand evidence and supporting references before publishing.

## Required workflow for an authority article

1. Define one primary search question/topic and one clear audience.
2. Identify the search intent: informational, comparison, local/service, commercial research or after-purchase care.
3. Write a useful excerpt that answers why the page matters.
4. Build a substantive body with clear H2/H3 sections; the scoring model gives full body credit at 500+ words.
5. Add nursery-specific first-hand evidence: seasonal observation, batch behaviour, propagation/care result, customer question pattern, project outcome or photographed diagnostic example.
6. Name an expert reviewer/owner.
7. Add at least one credible source/reference URL when factual claims need external support.
8. Add a real hero image before major promotion; do not fabricate product photography.
9. Review the quality score in Admin; publishing is blocked below the configured threshold.
10. After publication, connect the article to the relevant collection/product pages and review performance before producing adjacent content.

## Priority content clusters

- Aglaonema care, colour retention and light selection.
- Alocasia leaf/yellowing/root-care diagnostics.
- ZZ / Raven ZZ low-light and watering guidance.
- Snake plants for homes/offices and pot-size selection.
- Fern humidity, placement and Pune summer/monsoon care.
- Low-light plants, AC-office plants and shaded-balcony plants.
- Beginner plant selection and missed-watering tolerance.
- Pune seasonal care: summer heat, monsoon drainage, winter watering.
- Corporate gifting and office greening case studies.
- Nursery arrivals / live-batch stories with real photography and current availability.

## Do not publish

- Mass-generated pages that only swap the plant name.
- Unsupported medical/toxicity/safety claims.
- Fake reviews, fake expert names, fabricated nursery observations or synthetic product photos presented as real inventory.
- Fixed stock/price claims that are not connected to a verified live SKU/batch.
